# Contribution guidelines

Thanks for suggesting a tool! To keep the list useful for business teams:

- **Open source or free to self-host.** Paid SaaS only if it has a genuinely free tier, and mark it `(freemium)`.
- **Actively maintained:** a commit or release in the last 6 months.
- **Useful to a non-developer team** in at least one department, or a framework many business tools are built on.
- **One tool per pull request**, added at the end of the right section.
- **Format:** `- [Name](link) — One sentence on what it does for a business.` No marketing words ("best", "revolutionary").
- Add your own project if it fits; say so in the pull request.

Not sure where it fits? Open an [issue](https://github.com/somakant/awesome-ai-agents-for-business/issues/new/choose) or a [discussion](https://github.com/somakant/awesome-ai-agents-for-business/discussions). Please follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
